Workflow

How it works

Simple for faculty, invisible for students. Entire roll-call in under 10 seconds.

STEP 01
Faculty signs in
Secure email + password login via Supabase. No complex onboarding required.
STEP 02
Capture classroom photo
Take a single group photo or use webcam. AI detects all faces instantly.
STEP 03
AI marks attendance
Faces matched against enrolled embeddings. Present / Absent marked in under 1.2s per student.
STEP 04
Export and close
Review, edit if needed, then export a clean CSV — also synced live to the student dashboard.
